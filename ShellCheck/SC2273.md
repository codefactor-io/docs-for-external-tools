Pattern: Sequence of `===`s found

Issue: -

## Description

A sequence of `=======` may be a border or separator that is missing the `#` needed to make it a comment. It may also be a marker left behind from an unresolved source control merge conflict. In that case, resolve the conflict and remove all of its markers.

Example of **incorrect** code:

```sh
<<<<<<< HEAD
echo "Goodbye World"
=======
echo "Hello World!"
>>>>>>> mybranch
```

Example of **correct** code:

```sh
# =======
```
